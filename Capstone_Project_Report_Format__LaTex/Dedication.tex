    \section*{Dedication}
\end{center}
\large We dedicate this work to our parents, who somehow kept believing in us even when
deadlines were brutal and progress felt invisible. Their support --- whether it was
a late-night call, a meal left at the door, or just a quiet ``you'll figure it out'' ---
made the whole thing possible. We also dedicate this to our supervisor, Sudipto Chaki Sir, whose patience and feedback
shaped not just this project but how we think about building things. And to every
doctor and patient who had to navigate a broken healthcare system while we were busy
trying to build a small fix for it --- this one is for you too.
