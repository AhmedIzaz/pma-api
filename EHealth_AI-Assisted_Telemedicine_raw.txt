E. Blockchain Consensus and Prescription Integrity
EHealth uses the Polygon Proof-of-Stake (PoS)
network as an external trust layer for prescription
verification. Blockchain consensus is the mechanism
through which distributed participants agree on the
valid state and ordering of transactions without relying
on a single centralized authority. Two widely used
consensus approaches are Proof of Work (PoW) and
Proof of Stake (PoS). PoW requires participating
miners to perform computationally expensive
hash-based work before a block can be proposed,
whereas PoS assigns consensus responsibilities to
validators based on staked assets and applies
economic incentives and penalties to encourage
protocol-compliant behavior.

VI. EVALUATION

To evaluate the accuracy of the automated clinical
report generation pipeline, we tested an end-to-end
evaluation of the automated speech recognition (ASR)
stage (Xenova/whisper-tiny) and the information
structured extraction stage (Baichuan-M2-32B
Model). A test set of 50 recorded physician-patient
consultation dialogues was transcribed using
whisper-tiny after conversion to 16 kHz mono WAV
format. The transcriptions were then passed to the
extraction model to generate the four core clinical
fields: Patient Symptoms, Doctor Diagnosis,
Prescribed Medications, and Follow-up Plan.
Ground truth for both transcription and extracted
entities was established via manual evaluation by a
medical professional.

A. Triage Classification Performance
To evaluate this, we compared the LLM-generated
triage levels against standard labeled data collected
from multiple genuine sources. For this we took 200
manually collected and labeled text prompts,
representing potential medical scenarios. The triage
classification was a three-point scale: HIGH
(Critical), MEDIUM (Urgent-Fever/Pain), and
LOW(Non-Urgent), alongside a 'Non-Medical'
category, which we treated as 'LOW' or excluded from
the core triage comparison, depending on the research
question. The classification results are detailed in the
confusion matrix and summarized performance
metrics. The absence of critical false negatives proves
